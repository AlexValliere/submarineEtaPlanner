# AI Usage Disclosure

Submarine ETA Planner was developed through active human–AI collaboration.

Using the terminology from the [Dalamud AI Usage Policy](https://dalamud.dev/plugin-publishing/ai-policy/), the appropriate disclosure level is **Copilot**:

- Alex Vallière defined the product goals, behavior, safety boundaries, route-model requirements, and visual direction.
- Alex reviewed the results in game, supplied real SubmarineTracker scenarios, identified incorrect behavior, and approved each workflow change.
- OpenAI Codex produced a substantial portion of the implementation, tests, documentation, and release-support changes under that direction.
- Alex remains responsible for understanding, testing, maintaining, and supporting the submitted code.

The installer icon in `images/icon.png` was generated with OpenAI image-generation tooling under Alex's direction. It is disclosed as an AI-generated asset in the plugin manifest. A future genuinely human-created replacement may be substituted without changing the plugin's behavior.
